👋🏻Hello, I'm Jiahui.

I am an undergraduate in Software Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), expected to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, large language models, and adaptive intelligent agents. I am particularly interested in:

  • Reinforcement learning for LLMs: post-training and test-time learning, with a focus on exploration, verification, and effective use of feedback.
  • Adaptive and self-improving agents: enabling agents to learn from interaction, reuse experience, and adapt to new tasks and changing environments.
  • Embodied AI: exploring learning and decision-making for autonomous agents in partially observed and dynamic physical environments.
